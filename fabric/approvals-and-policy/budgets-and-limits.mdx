> **Status:** Live partner-preview tasks pass server-owned fail-closed funding
> reservation and settlement authority. Caller-defined step, time, spend, model,
> tool, and approval limits are unavailable.

## Current authority

The authenticated personal tenant and server admission path choose the task's
funding authority. The public handler rejects the `policy` field, including
`maxSpendMicros`, risk ceilings, model lanes, and approval modes. A key scope is
an endpoint permission; it is not a customer-defined budget.

An admitted paid task has a reservation before execution and authoritative
settlement afterward. `GET /v1/usage` reads completed provider and settled
retail evidence. It does **not** report reserved spend or replace reservation,
settlement, reconciliation, or invoice authority.

## Public outcome projection

| Internal outcome | Public v1 projection |
|---|---|
| verified completion | `completed` / `run.completed` |
| ordinary failure, partial, or expiry | `failed` / `run.failed` with `run_failed` |
| confirmed cancellation | `cancelled` / `run.cancelled` |
| reconciliation in progress | `running` / non-terminal `run.progress` |
| unverifiable committing outcome | `failed` / `run.failed` with `run_outcome_unknown`, `retryable:false` |

The cancellation-reconciliation migration and compatible Workers are deployed.
The last two final-status behaviors remain pending a separate live canary. Once
that gate clears, the exact no-retry message during reconciliation is:

```text
The run is reconciling an uncertain execution outcome. Do not retry or resubmit it.
```

Successful recovery emits:

```text
Run reconciliation completed; execution is resuming.
```

An unknown terminal outcome uses:

```text
A committing action may have happened, but the final outcome could not be verified. Do not retry automatically.
```

Do not retry or resubmit based on a non-terminal cancellation response. A
cancellation request is cooperative and cannot roll back an already committed
action.

## Roadmap controls

Customer-configurable immutable step/time/spend ceilings, organization pooling,
per-key budgets, policy packs, and request-level tightening remain planned.
Their SDK schema fields are reserved contracts, not active behavior.
