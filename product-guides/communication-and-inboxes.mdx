Use Praxa across agent email, unified inbox triage, and operational task or invoice views.

<Note>
Account availability and admitted provider actions are checked at runtime. Drafting, sending, labeling, and archiving are different authorities.
</Note>

## Provided capabilities

| Capability | Status | Surfaces | Who it helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agent email**<br />Use a Praxa-owned email address for agent send-and-receive workflows where account delivery is available. | Live | iOS, web | Individual, Business, Team |
| **Inbox One**<br />Triage multiple connected inboxes from one agent-assisted view. | Live | iOS, web | Individual, Business, Team |
| **Agent Hub**<br />Inspect agent tasks, execution status, sales items, invoices, and related events. | Live | iOS, web | Individual, Business, Team |

## Example outcomes

1. **Triage connected inboxes.** Group new messages by urgency and inspect the account associated with every result.
2. **Draft before sending.** Ask for a reply draft and confirm no message sends until the exact send action is approved.
3. **Inspect an agent task.** Open Agent Hub and follow a task from its current state to its linked evidence.
4. **Review an invoice exception.** Inspect extracted invoice details and keep payment or ledger mutation outside the review step.
