Research products, meals, travel, and connected-device actions while keeping purchase or control consequences explicit.

<Note>
Research and preparation do not imply autonomous purchase authority. AgentCard remains beta, and travel booking uses an explicit handoff.
</Note>

## Provided capabilities

| Capability | Status | Surfaces | Who it helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AgentCard purchases**<br />Pilot approval-gated purchases through single-use virtual card authority in the supported environment. | Beta | iOS, web | Individual, Business |
| **Shopping and deals**<br />Research products, compare options, and prepare carts or shopping lists. | Live | iOS, web | Individual, Business |
| **Food ordering and meal planning**<br />Search menus, plan meals, and use approval-gated handoff for supported ordering flows. | Live | iOS, web | Individual, Business, Team |
| **Travel planning**<br />Research and compare flights, stays, and itineraries while leaving booking to an explicit handoff. | Live | iOS, web | Individual, Business, Team |
| **Smart home control**<br />Inspect and control connected SmartThings or Home Assistant devices through supported actions. | Live | iOS, web, voice | Individual |

## Example outcomes

1. **Compare products.** Ask for a criteria-based comparison and inspect the cited price and availability sources.
2. **Plan a meal.** Build a meal plan and use the supported approval-gated ordering handoff if available.
3. **Prepare a trip.** Compare itinerary options, then complete booking yourself through the provided handoff.
4. **Control a device.** Ask for the current smart-home state, then approve the exact supported change.

## Verify the surface

1. Confirm the capability status and listed surface in the [Capability Atlas](/overview/capabilities).
2. Sign in to the intended Praxa product surface and select the correct personal or organization workspace.
3. Exercise the example with the smallest safe scope and inspect the visible result or approval card.
4. Test one unavailable, denied, or disconnected state so the surface fails honestly.
