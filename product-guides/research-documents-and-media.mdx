Use Praxa to gather current evidence and turn it into documents, media, presentations, or interactive artifacts.

<Note>
Generated artifacts and research outputs require inspection. A rendered result is not independent verification of every underlying claim.
</Note>

## Example outcomes

1. **Research a current decision.** Ask for a multi-source comparison and inspect every cited source before using the conclusion.
2. **Turn evidence into a document.** Generate a document from supplied sources and download the resulting artifact.
3. **Create a presentation.** Request a concise deck, then inspect slide copy, media attribution, and export.
4. **Build an interactive explainer.** Describe a small interactive experience and test the published controls on compact and wide screens.
